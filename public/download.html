Get Paluwagan

Run your paluwagan on any phone or computer.

Android

Your device

Download the app and install it directly. No Play Store needed.

Download APK
  1. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.
  2. If Android blocks it, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back and install.

iPhone and iPad

Your device

Add Paluwagan to your home screen. It opens full-screen like an app.

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Windows and Mac

Your device

Install it as an app with its own window, Start menu entry and taskbar icon.

  1. Open this page in Microsoft Edge or Chrome.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or in Edge open ⋯ → Apps → Install this site as an app.
  3. Click Install.

Installed. Open Paluwagan from your Start menu or taskbar.
